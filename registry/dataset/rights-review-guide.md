# Rights-review groups withheld from the public reuse inventory

The source is an aggregate DigitalNZ rights facet. Counts are weighted record
counts, not a claim that each individual item has been manually reviewed.

## Permission required or explicit restriction

These statements expressly limit reuse: private-study-only terms, prior
written permission, cultural permissions, donor permission, or no copying and
exhibition. Examples include Fairfax private-study terms and an Alexander
Turnbull Library requirement to obtain permission before reuse. Treat all
items in this group as **do not redistribute or transform without a current
provider permission check**.

## Copyright reserved or all rights reserved

This group includes `All rights reserved`, copyright-protected statements, and
rights that vary by publication date. It is not an open-licence group. The
rights statement and source link may be retained as metadata, but the linked
object itself should not be copied to a public derivative archive without a
specific provider licence or a verified statutory exception.

## Unresolved, some rights reserved, or other

This group includes `Some rights reserved` and opaque provider legal-statement
or catalogue URLs. It does not establish a permissive licence. Resolve the
provider's current terms at item level before moving an entry into a public
reuse group.

## Decision rule

The public archive contains aggregate rights metadata only. Even an approved
licence label must be checked against the original provider record before
redistributing an underlying object, thumbnail, transcript, or media file.
